# Full Closure-Derived Interface

## 1. Interface principle

Uniface is not a database that stores notes beneath a fixed ontology. It is a source-preserving latent field whose organization changes through understanding, interpretation, and interaction.

```text
source note
↔ understood candidate relation
↔ explicit interpretation
↔ interaction and challenge
↔ configured admission
↔ provisional return
↔ transformed note or rule
↔ reopening
```

Closure is the continuity of this entire loop. It is neither supplied before the note field nor declared terminal after a return.

## 2. Canonical depths

### Literal source

Exact text, equations, drawings, dates, and context remain immutable.

### Axiometric operator field

Notes are indexed by the literal operations they enact:

```text
0↔∞
r/i
Triangle Time
implicit shell return
Chaitin–Kakeya
tan(π/2) fold
predual Fourier
four-i
ball–hair
loop–sensor–selection
metavectorization
```

### Candidate relation depth

Semantic and operator resemblance propose possible correspondences without asserting identity.

### Interpretation depth

A translation witness records frame, scope, preservation, transformation, omission, status, and reverse path.

### Configuration depth

Interaction decides which interpretations are admitted, revised, rejected, or OPEN. The current configuration records coherence, covering scope, saturation policy, rules, evidence, and affected perspectives.

### Returned projection

Black Mirror displays the current topology of the configured admission without claiming to reconstruct every source path or hidden residue.

### Reopening

Every return can become the point, assumption, note, or sensor condition of another interaction.

## 4. Configured unification

The existence of mathematical forms does not automatically establish their intended unity.

```text
mathematical availability
≠ configured unification
```

Understanding proposes possible operational correspondences. Interpretation makes one correspondence definite. Interaction challenges and configures its admission.

A returned unity is admitted only when its configured relation passes the required coherence, covering, saturation, source, frame, status, and contradiction conditions.

The returned unity remains linked to the exact configuration that forced it and remains reopenable.

See [`CONFIGURED_UNIFICATION.md`](CONFIGURED_UNIFICATION.md).

## 5. Slearn

Slearn is the active recursive configuration continuum. It supports:

- entering exact notes;
- retrieving distant formulations;
- proposing candidate relations;
- tracing `r`-extension and `i`-rotation;
- inspecting `0↔∞` inversions;
- comparing unresolved variants;
- authoring interpretation witnesses;
- challenging compositions and frames;
- accepting, revising, rejecting, or reopening relations;
- following sensor–selection cycles;
- constructing relation-derived communities and metavectors;
- connecting Lean theorems to exact readings;
- recording conscious cultural and moral consequences;
- reopening every returned form.

Questions, lessons, projects, communities, and tokens are derived interaction roles rather than isolated primitives.

## 6. Black Mirror

Black Mirror is the nonterminal topological appearance of the current configured admission.

It may display:

- returned classes and loops;
- ball–hair relations;
- path invariants and hidden order;
- perspective and gluing obstructions;
- sensor–selection boundaries;
- candidate, admitted, rejected, and OPEN relations;
- competing configurations over the same mathematical forms;
- which distinctions the projection deletes;
- formal, simulated, empirical, hypothetical, cultural, and moral status.

Selecting a displayed form opens its exact notes, candidate relations, interpretation witnesses, accepted and rejected alternatives, affected perspectives, rule versions, and reopening paths.

## 7. Conscious cultural morality

The foundational field is culture, morality, and consciousness in interaction. Money, AI, rules, law, and social order are downstream views.

Interpretive configuration is an act of authorship because it changes what the interface later retrieves, connects, privileges, and projects.

The interface must show:

- who and what sensed a condition;
- who proposed and confirmed an interpretation;
- who could select or respond;
- who authored the returned form;
- what consequences returned;
- which perspectives were hidden;
- whether the field can correct and reopen.

A stable, mathematically familiar, or widely shared configuration does not automatically constitute moral or translational truth.

## 8. Computation

Uniface does not assume Turing completeness.

```text
halt ↔ selected point
continuation ↔ reopened trajectory
```

A digital machine can realize one local form. The interface scopes every computational theorem to its language or machine and shows what the halt reopens.

## 9. Translational truth

```text
OPEN  = coherent translation remains unfinished
FALSE = translated forms contradict
TRUE  = the required relation provisionally returns
```

Truth is not a label attached before interaction and not a terminal judgment after it. It is what remains mutually translatable through the configured active relation.

A TRUE return identifies a provisional unity forced by its admission. It does not declare that no alternative configuration or later reopening exists.

## 13. Non-negotiable rule

The interface is complete only as active continuity. It must never:

- assume closure before translation;
- use closure as terminal;
- assume Turing completeness;
- replace notes with embeddings;
- treat downstream systems as the foundational network;
- treat mathematical availability as intended unification;
- hide source, interpretation, configuration, path, perspective, or status.
