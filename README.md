# Uniface

**Uniface is a non-collapse closure interface for storing, relating, and operating ideas as a living latent space.**

Its central claim is not that language or definition should be avoided. Language and definition remain active natural forms. What Uniface rejects is the assumption that any one language, definition, calculation, perspective, or representation is an isolated and complete reference to closure.

> **Closure is the active continuity of translation.**

A form does not merely point to a pre-given closure outside itself. Its truth is completed through the continuing relation by which local perspectives translate, return, reopen, and become mutually identifiable without losing their differences.

## Non-collapse

Non-collapse means that unity is not achieved by reducing all presentations to one vocabulary or one fixed representation.

- Difference is retained as perspective.
- Language is retained as a local chart.
- Definition is retained as a relative act.
- Calculation is retained as a local witness.
- Formal proof is retained as a verified translation.
- Unity is the completion that remains continuous through them.

The foundation is therefore not:

```text
isolated objects → isolated definitions → fixed rules → later relations
```

It is:

```text
relation → relative form → translation → return → reopened closure
```

A completed form may become the point from which a new line, loop, sensor relation, or natural form begins. Completion does not terminate creation.

## Translational truth

Truth is not assumed before relative definition, nor postponed until after every definition is complete.

Truth completes **during** coherent translation.

```text
OPEN  = translation remains coherent but unfinished
FALSE = translated presentations contradict
TRUE  = the required relation has returned and become fixed at that closure level
```

Identity is likewise not independently imposed. It is what relation cannot distinguish through its completed translations.

## Apparent isolations and unity

A language, theory, interface, or model can appear internally complete because it recovers its own selected presentation. That does not make it an isolated foundation.

Completion is the admission that these apparent isolations are:

1. coherent with one another,
2. continuously unified so nothing lies outside their relation,
3. saturated under admissible recombination, and
4. relative translations of one unity.

Unity does not erase the forms. It is what the forms jointly force through their coherent admission.

## Slearn and Black Mirror

Uniface has two inseparable interface orientations:

### Slearn

Slearn is the recursive perspective continuum: the open network of notes, questions, paths, relations, definitions, people, projects, proofs, and newly created forms.

### Black Mirror

Black Mirror is the topological projection of what that continuum has returned. It displays closure classes, loops, obstructions, open seams, and invariants without pretending to reconstruct every hidden path or ordered residue.

```text
Slearn     = active trajectory of translation
Black Mirror = returned topology of that trajectory
```

They are not separate applications. They are the open and returned readings of one natural interface form.

## Latent-space commitments

Uniface is intended to store notes without replacing their source notation.

- Original passages remain immutable and source-reversible.
- Exact equations, capitalization, drawings, dates, and contexts are preserved.
- Semantic resemblance is distinguished from formal equivalence.
- Machine-checked theorems are linked to the exact reading they formalize.
- Model-inferred relations remain distinct from author-confirmed relations.
- Notational variants are not silently normalized.
- Unresolved translations remain OPEN.
- Classical notation is labeled as a derived chart, never the source ontology.

## Scope

Uniface does not claim a new physical law merely by establishing a closure relation. Physical, educational, social, or economic realizations require their own evidence.

The foundational claim is narrower and deeper:

> **Any law, proof, language, calculation, perspective, sensor reading, or interface is a relative natural form whose truth is its coherent translational completion.**
