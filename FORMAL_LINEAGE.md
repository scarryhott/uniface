## Interface interpretation

The formal sequence supports:

```text
literal source axiometry
→ explicit reading
→ formal natural form
→ theorem
→ source-reversible interpretation
→ reopening
```

```text
classical formalism replaces source grammar
Turing chart becomes universal foundation
formal closure becomes physical law
return becomes terminal closure
```
